⚡ Its Impact

With travel, there is again a problem, is that the local cultures remain intact, opposing globalisation's interconnectedness, with problems rooted in xenophobia. This is no joke, when somebody is transporting their "life" for a service, they can start with less opportunities because of their color, gender or beliefs.

A study suggest from 2022 that, the acceptance rate of people, depending on their skin color changes. For instance, a white females has an acceptance rate of 94% whilst black males are down at 89%.(Ridgeway_). This isn't just a weird number, primary sources support the idea of discrimination...For example, Sohail Inayatullah, working in higher education, experienced his identity being questioned: "it was this look that the examiner asked me about next. She asked me what type of skin complexion I had. "Brown," I had written on the citizenship form. "No. The only categories we have are fair, medium, and dark." "Well, I am not dark and I am not fair." She wanted to argue that I was dark. My being medium made her color problematic, since she was not fair and she was clearly not as dark as me. We both fought for the middle spot, with her finally relenting. " .( University of Hawai'i Press; ). This makes people uncomfortable, as they are literally debating about who they are. The weird custom described earlier wasn't just weird, it had councquenes: "...once the FBI stalked me, thinking I was part of an operation selling passports/drugs to and from Indonesia"

🔧 Problems & Solutions

Local cultures do persist, but it doesn't have to be negative! Sources, (Britannica.) and (Ratnam, Karthiga ), suggest that, stopping intergenerational racism, involves changing education, the personal experiences and the outside effects, to shape ones's beliefs. This means that through education or environment switch, peoples beliefs, or even their xenophobia can be changed. This is supported by a study, by (Villavicencio, Adriana, et al. ), where a school with a majority of white males in higher positions and lower positions with minorities , showed that through educating the staff, the behavior and beliefs can change in a positive way. This supports Karthringa's hypothesis, because it already shows improvements because of education.
