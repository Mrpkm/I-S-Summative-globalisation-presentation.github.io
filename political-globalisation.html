📖 Overview

At the end of WW2, the world was devastated. Having fallen back to 100 year levels in trade, the world was starting a new era. (Vanham, Peter.) This period marked the start of the US trade influence, with the USA providing technologies, like cars and planes, to aid the second industrial revolution. . (Vanham, Peter.)

Institutions like the European Union, funded the economic recovery. Through their free trade, international trade rose again. By 1989 the economic levels once again accounted for the ones in 1914, with a sudden increase of middle class income. (Vanham, Peter.)

On the contrary, the world saw a grade divide. After the liberation of Europe in 1945, the soviet union spread its influence on the eastern states, establishing left-wing governments in various states.J.E. Luebering.

The former allies however, Britain and the US, were in fear of eastern Europe remaining under influence of the soviet union. The divided states of western Europe, with the USA, formed the North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO) , a unified army, to resist Soviet efforts in the control of Europe.J.E. Luebering.

Later this resulted in the polarization of the world—a process in which groups, who share different views, develop extreme versions of each other's beliefs. (Wyland, Jessica.)

The two polars of the world, the US and the Soviet, influence sphere, clustered into different areas. These groups founded alliances and raced with each other, in the mentality of "We vs them" (Wyland, Jessica.) This mentality affected the cultural, economic and social state of the world…

⚡ Its Impact

The social impact of the cold war was the great divide it has achieved. For instance, new terms were coined to determine which country was part of which group. The terms were:

1st world countries were part of the industrialized west, integrated into the influence of the USA. (nationsonline)

2nd world countries were part of the communist east, where the oppressed workers had in theory, their power. (nationsonline)

3rd world countries were considered the neutral or the part of the block, which did not participate in the cold war actively. (nationsonline)

The nations that were not actively engaging to protect one of the groups ( third world countries) have been influenced in efforts to bring more and more fighters to the ideological cold war. J.E. Luebering. The term also showed, how the people lived their everyday life…

The cultural impact was the fear people had about second world countries. About this time, media and propaganda spread horrific ideas about what the soviets are capable of. (Landwehr, Dominik.) People's fear was mainly about being brainwashed into believing the other side's opinion—so much so that Swiss historian Jean-Rudolf von Salis (1901-1996) described it as an anxiety psychosis. (Landwehr, Dominik.)

The economic impact, caused by the cold war, transformed the internal structure of the countries. For instance, defence spending skyrocketed to the point, where a countries had to manage their economic growth with their military. (Emre Alkin)

This is all due to the 2 opinions dividing the world at that time. J.E. Luebering. The fear which leads countries to purchase and use significant portion of their economy solely on their military, has also, in term, caused them to fear even more the other side's attack , chasing the countries to a cycle of spending.

🔧 Problems & Solutions

It almost seems that the fear of each other's opinions was the catalyst of every effect. The constant fear didn't just separate millions if not billions of people, but it has also damaged the mental health of them, which lead to increasment in military spending. Which again , fueled fear of the cycle.(Landwehr, Dominik.)

So what ended the cold war? After years of economic recession, Mikhail Gorbachev was chosen to lead the soviet union.(Nye, Joseph S. ) He set out to bring back the country's economy. First through hard work, but later through encouragement .(Nye, Joseph S. )

He listened to the opinion of the people, they wanted to "get out". So he started forming closer ties with the USA. He broke the 40 year thinking, started revolutionizing the established thinking of the time: "'New thinking should, at long last, enter the sphere of our economic relations," (Gorbachev) (NewYork Times editors circa 1988)

Over time, American brands such as McDonalds (Bisharyan, Julietta ) got into the soviet union, and people felt less and less scared of each other.

Soon the borders opened, families re-united in Germany.(Nye, Joseph S. ) , and the soviet union officially collapsed at december 26 1991.
